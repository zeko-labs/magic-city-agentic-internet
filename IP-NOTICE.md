## Conceptual Positioning

Magic City is a proof-bearing internet-agent commerce layer. It converts user intent into scoped agent-executable work, selects or hires agents, applies payment and privacy policy, coordinates execution, and returns artifacts with receipts and settlement/proof state.

upstream-derived components retain their original license notices.
